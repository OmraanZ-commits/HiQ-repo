Lead promotional videos with a business problem and actual navigation through the running portal, using original campaign imagery and music as supporting transitions rather than replacing the application with abstract diagrams.

**Why:** The earlier diagram-heavy film was explicitly rejected because it looked like boxes instead of showing how the app works. The requested replacement combines engaging business-context storytelling with substantial footage of real navigation.

**How to apply:** When producing future portal marketing videos, capture genuine screens and interactions rather than approximating the UI. When featuring ATP, distinguish browser-generated exports and deterministic demonstration examples from live data or SAP writes, both on screen and in narration.
