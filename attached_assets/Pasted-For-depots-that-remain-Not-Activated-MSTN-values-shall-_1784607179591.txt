For depots that remain Not Activated:
•	MSTN values shall continue to be refreshed and monitored.
•	When MSTN clearance is subsequently achieved, the depot shall become eligible for activation.
•	The system shall raise an activation action for NCSM review.
•	NCSM may approve activation of the newly eligible depot without impacting already activated depots.
The activation of an additional depot shall update downstream CCR mappings accordingly.
7. UI requirement
Per CBU
The system shall display:
•	CBU Identifier
•	Overall Activation Status (Draft / Partially Active / Active)
•	List of Invoicing Depots
Per Depot
The system shall display:
•	Depot Code
•	MSTN %
•	MSTN Cleared Indicator (Yes / No)
•	NCSM Decision (Approved / Rejected / Pending)
•	Activation Status (Active / Not Activated)
•	Activation Indicator (✓ / ✕)
•	Select for Activation checkbox/toggle (available only during approval)
Activation Indicator Rules
Activation Indicator	Meaning
✓	Depot approved by NCSM and active for execution
✕	Depot not approved by NCSM and excluded from execution
☐	Pending approval decision
The Activation Indicator shall remain visible after approval to provide continuous visibility of depot-level activation decisions.
The Activation Indicator shall drive downstream CCR eligibility:
•	✓ Depots shall be passed to CCR.
•	✕ Depots shall not be passed to CCR.
•	☐ Depots shall remain pending until approval is completed.
Example – Partial Activation
CBU	Invoicing Depot	MSTN %	MSTN Cleared	NCSM Decision	Activation Indicator	Activation Status
CBU-001	DLGH	92%	Yes	Approved	✓	Active
CBU-001	VABH	88%	Yes	Approved	✓	Active
CBU-001	VJDH	63%	No	Rejected	✕	Not Activated
Result
•	Basepack Status = Partially Active
•	DLGH and VABH are available in CCR.
•	VJDH is excluded from CCR.
•	Activation Indicators displayed: 
o	DLGH = ✓
o	VABH = ✓
o	VJDH = ✕
Subsequent MSTN Clearance Example
CBU	Invoicing Depot	MSTN %	MSTN Cleared	NCSM Decision	Activation Indicator	Activation Status
CBU-001	VJDH	84%	Yes	Pending	☐	Not Activated
After NCSM approval:
CBU	Invoicing Depot	MSTN %	MSTN Cleared	NCSM Decision	Activation Indicator	Activation Status
CBU-001	VJDH	84%	Yes	Approved	✓	Active
Result:
•	VJDH becomes available in CCR.
•	Basepack transitions from Partially Active to Active if all remaining depots are activated.
•	Activation of VJDH does not require reactivation of already approved depots.
