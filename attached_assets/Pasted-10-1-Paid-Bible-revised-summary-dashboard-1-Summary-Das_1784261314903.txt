10.1 Paid Bible revised summary dashboard
1.	Summary Dashboard overview 
•	The Summary Dashboard shall provide a consolidated, actionable view of basepack status, service requests, network readiness, and operational risks, enabling users to monitor overall health and navigate to detailed views for execution.
2.	Pack Summary & Status View
•	System shall display total basepacks and their distribution across statuses:
o	Active
o	Draft
o	Inactive
o	Seasonal
o	Stock Base Delisted / Delisted 
•	View shall be segmented by Small C category  

3.	 Service Request & Amendment Tracker
o	Dashboard shall display request-wise summary for:
	Geo Extension
	Channel Extension
	Customer Extension
	Pricing Issues
o	For each request type, system shall display:
	Count of Pending / Approved / Rejected
	Total requests
	Aging (on interaction) 
o	On user interaction (double click), system shall:
	Show detailed request list
	Display full lifecycle and history
o	Aging and status tracking is required to ensure traceability and operational follow-up.
4.	SLOB Risk & Network Exposure
o	Dashboard shall show:
	SLOB risk at Small C level
	Count of CBU × DC combinations at risk
	Quantity (volume/value) using MRP basis
o	Users shall be able to:
	Drill down to Small C level detail
	Analyse exposure by depot/network
5.	Relaunch & Network Buckets
	Dashboard shall display relaunch buckets:
	N+15, N+30, N+60 timelines
	For upcoming network relaunches grouped into: 
	0–15 days
	16–30 days
	30–60 days
	Each bucket shall show:
	Grammage
	MRP
	Artwork
	CC
	CP
	Silent Changes
	Drilldown to basepack detail at Small C level
6.	Dispatch Start Tracking (ITR Stock in Pipeline Networks)
	Dashboard shall provide visibility into dispatch start timelines (ITR stock entering pipeline networks).
	Dispatch readiness shall be bucketed into:
	0–15 days
	16–30 days
	30–60 days
	Each bucket shall show:
	Grammage
	MRP
	Artwork
	CC
	CP
	Silent Changes
	Drilldown to basepack detail at Small C level
7.	MSTN & Launch Readiness Indicators
	Dashboard shall provide MSTN readiness visibility at MOC level, with a dropdown to select the relevant MOC (e.g., MOC 5, MOC 6, etc.).
	For the selected MOC, system shall display readiness at Small C level across the following categories:
	MSTN Cleared
	MSTN Not Cleared
	Exception Clearance
	Each category shall represent the count of basepack / CBU combinations falling under the respective status.
10.2	Paid Bible – Dashboard – Multi select filter
The Summary Dashboard shall provide multi-select filter capability across key dimensions of the product and distribution hierarchy, including:
•	BG (Business Group)
•	Sales Category
•	Small C
•	Format
•	Pack Status
•	Channel
•	Sub Channel 1
•	Sub Channel 2
•	Customer
