20.5 Auto-CCR MT Listing Optimization & CBU Recommendation Engine
Description
The CCR application shall support an Auto-CCR recommendation engine for MT customers that evaluates currently mapped relaunch CBUs and alternate listing candidates using ATP availability, pricing readiness, allocation outcomes, freshness criteria, and inventory signals. The recommendation engine shall leverage customer-specific freshness rules maintained within the BSS Master to ensure that only fresh inventory is considered during recommendation generation. 
The objective is to ensure customer orders are mapped to the most appropriate CBU while minimising allocation losses, reducing order failures, and improving successor CBU adoption during relaunch transitions. The recommendation generated by the engine shall be used as an input for downstream activities. 
Functional Requirements
1. Applicable Scope
The recommendation engine shall be applicable for:
•	Relaunch networks
•	Predecessor–Successor transitions
•	MT customers
•	Customer chains participating in Auto-CCR processes
The logic shall not apply to new launch CBUs. 
2. Candidate CBU Identification
The system shall evaluate:
•	Currently Mapped CBU
•	Alternate CBU 1
•	Alternate CBU 2
•	Alternate CBU 3
•	Alternate CBU n
Candidate CBUs shall be derived from:
•	MT Listing master data
•	Successor–Predecessor relationships
The system shall maintain the sequence of alternate CBUs available for evaluation. 
3. ATP Validation
The system shall retrieve ATP availability for:
•	Mapped CBU
•	All Alternate CBUs
ATP shall be evaluated after completion of the latest ECC allocation cycle.
ATP values shall be retrieved at:
CBU × Depot × Batch level.
The ATP values shall be displayed to users and used for recommendation generation. 
4. Pricing Validation
Prior to freshness evaluation and recommendation generation, the system shall validate whether each candidate CBU is commercially deployable.
Validation shall include:
•	Price condition availability
•	Pricing activation status
A CBU failing pricing validation shall:
•	Be excluded from recommendation processing
•	Be tagged as "Price Not Active"
Example:
CBU	ATP	Price Check
CBU1	10	Valid
CBU2	100	Valid
CBU3	50	Invalid
Only CBU1 and CBU2 shall proceed to freshness evaluation. CBU3 shall be excluded from recommendation generation. 
The system shall rank alternate CBUs based on ATP availability
5. Freshness Validation
Following pricing validation, the system shall evaluate freshness using customer-specific criteria maintained in the BSS Master.
The validation shall utilise the following BSS fields:
•	Customer
•	Material
•	Valid Day from Prod Date
Freshness shall be evaluated at:
Customer Code × CBU × Depot × Batch
For each inventory batch:
Stock Age = Current Date – Production Date
The system shall compare the calculated Stock Age against the Valid Day from Prod Date maintained in the BSS Master.
If:
Stock Age ≤ Valid Day from Prod Date
the inventory shall be considered Fresh and ATP from the batch shall be eligible for recommendation processing.
If:
Stock Age > Valid Day from Prod Date
the inventory shall be considered Non-Fresh and ATP from the batch shall be excluded from recommendation processing. 
Example – Freshness Evaluation
BSS Master
Customer	Material	Valid Day from Prod Date
10C331	TKFE8R5	108 Days
ATP Inventory
Customer Code	CBU	Depot	Batch	Stock Age (Days)	ATP
10C331	TKFE8R5	CHKH	B001	80	400
10C331	TKFE8R5	CHKH	B002	120	300
Evaluation Result for 10C331 X TKFE8R5:
Batch	Freshness Status	ATP 
B001	Fresh	400
B002	Non-Fresh	300
Fresh ATP for recommendation generation = 400. 
6. Current Mapped CBU Evaluation
The system shall evaluate whether the currently mapped CBU remains suitable for ordering.
Inputs shall include:
•	Pricing Validation Status
•	Fresh ATP Availability
•	ATP Threshold
Where Fresh ATP remains above configured thresholds, the current mapped CBU may continue to be recommended.
Where Fresh ATP falls below threshold levels, alternate CBU evaluation shall begin. 
7. Alternate CBU Ranking
The system shall rank alternate CBUs using the following sequence:
1.	Pricing Eligibility
2.	Fresh ATP Availability
3.	In-Transit Inventory Availability (Fallback)
Only CBUs passing pricing validation shall participate in Fresh ATP evaluation.
Fresh ATP shall be calculated as:
Sum of ATP quantities from all batches satisfying freshness criteria.
The alternate CBU with the highest Fresh ATP shall be identified as the preferred replacement candidate
Example – Alternate CBU Ranking
CBU	Pricing Active	Raw ATP	Fresh ATP
Current CBU	Yes	500	500
Alternate CBU 1	No	900	N/A
Alternate CBU 2	Yes	700	700
Alternate CBU 3	Yes	1200	N/A (No fresh ATP available)
Recommendation Ranking
Rank	CBU	Fresh ATP
1	Alternate CBU 2	700
2	Current CBU	500
3	Alternate CBU 3	N/A (No fresh ATP available)

Alternate CBU 1 is excluded due to inactive pricing.
Alternate CBU 2 shall be recommended because it has the highest eligible Fresh ATP availability. 
8. Allocation Dependency
The recommendation engine shall execute using ATP data generated after ECC allocation processing.
The system shall consume ATP information only after allocation completion to ensure that recommendations are based on actual inventory availability rather than theoretical stock positions. 
9. In-Transit Inventory Validation
Where Fresh ATP is unavailable across all evaluated CBUs, the system shall evaluate in-transit inventory.
In-transit inventory shall be consumed at:
CBU × Receiving Plant
The CBU with the highest valid in-transit availability shall be considered as a recommendation candidate when no eligible Fresh ATP exists. 
10. Auto-CCR Recommendation Generation
Based on Pricing Validation, Fresh ATP Evaluation and In-Transit Inventory validation, the system shall generate one of the following outcomes:
Scenario A – Retain Existing Mapping
Current Mapped CBU remains suitable.
Action:
•	No CBU shift required
Scenario B – Recommend Alternate CBU
An alternate CBU is available with superior Fresh ATP readiness.
Action:
•	Recommended CBU generated for replacement
Scenario C – No Eligible CBU Available
Conditions:
•	Fresh ATP = 0 across all evaluated CBUs
•	In-transit inventory unavailable across all evaluated CBUs
•	Pricing validation failure across all candidate CBUs
Action:
•	Flag for manual review
