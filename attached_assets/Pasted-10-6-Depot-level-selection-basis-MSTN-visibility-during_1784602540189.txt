10.6	Depot level selection (basis MSTN visibility) during basepack activation 
During Draft → Active activation, the system shall enable depot-level activation decisions at the CBU level. Activation eligibility shall be evaluated using MSTN values of the associated Invoicing Depots (Sourcing Depots).
1. CBU-wise Depot Visibility
A basepack may be associated with multiple CBUs.
Each CBU may be associated with one or more Invoicing Depots used for fulfilment.
The system shall display:
•	CBU-wise grouping
•	Associated Invoicing Depots
•	Depot-level activation status
•	Depot-level MSTN status
2. MSTN Visibility
For each Invoicing Depot, the system shall display:
•	Depot Code
•	Current MSTN %
•	MSTN Cleared Indicator (Yes / No)
•	Activation Status (Activated / Not Activated)
•	Date of first MSTN clearance
Once a depot achieves MSTN ≥ 80% during the evaluation cycle, the depot shall be treated as MSTN Cleared for activation purposes and the clearance status shall be retained.
3. Partial Activation Decision
The NCSM approver shall be able to activate:
•	All depots
•	A subset of depots
•	No depots
for a given CBU.
The activation decision shall be performed at depot level.
Depots selected for activation shall become Active for that basepack.
Depots not selected shall remain Not Activated.
4. Basepack Activation Status Determination
The system shall derive basepack activation status as follows:
Scenario	Basepack Status
All eligible depots activated	Active
Only a subset of depots activated	Partially Active
No depots activated	Draft
A Partially Active basepack shall remain operational only for approved depots.
5. Downstream CCR Impact
Only depots approved during activation shall be:
•	Passed to CCR
•	Available for customer code mapping
•	Available for CIC generation
•	Available for MT listing 
Depots that are not activated shall:
•	Not be passed to CCR
•	Appear as non-serviceable for the basepack
•	Not generate downstream mappings
6. Subsequent Depot Activation
For depots that remain Not Activated:
•	MSTN values shall continue to be refreshed and monitored.
•	When MSTN clearance is subsequently achieved, the depot shall become eligible for activation.
•	The system shall raise an activation action for NCSM review.
•	NCSM may approve activation of the newly eligible depot without impacting already activated depots.
The activation of an additional depot shall update downstream CCR mappings accordingly.
7. UI requirement
Per CBU
The system shall display:
•	CBU Identifier
•	Overall Activation Status (Draft / Partially Active / Active)
•	List of Invoicing Depots
Per Depot
The system shall display:
•	Depot Code
•	MSTN %
•	MSTN Cleared Indicator (Yes / No)
•	NCSM Decision (Approved / Rejected / Pending)
•	Activation Status (Active / Not Activated)
•	Activation Indicator (✓ / ✕)
•	Select for Activation checkbox/toggle (available only during approval)
Activation Indicator Rules
Activation Indicator	Meaning
✓	Depot approved by NCSM and active for execution
✕	Depot not approved by NCSM and excluded from execution
☐	Pending approval decision
The Activation Indicator shall remain visible after approval to provide continuous visibility of depot-level activation decisions.
The Activation Indicator shall drive downstream CCR eligibility:
•	✓ Depots shall be passed to CCR.
•	✕ Depots shall not be passed to CCR.
•	☐ Depots shall remain pending until approval is completed.
Example – Partial Activation
CBU	Invoicing Depot	MSTN %	MSTN Cleared	NCSM Decision	Activation Indicator	Activation Status
CBU-001	DLGH	92%	Yes	Approved	✓	Active
CBU-001	VABH	88%	Yes	Approved	✓	Active
CBU-001	VJDH	63%	No	Rejected	✕	Not Activated
Result
•	Basepack Status = Partially Active
•	DLGH and VABH are available in CCR.
•	VJDH is excluded from CCR.
•	Activation Indicators displayed: 
o	DLGH = ✓
o	VABH = ✓
o	VJDH = ✕
Subsequent MSTN Clearance Example
CBU	Invoicing Depot	MSTN %	MSTN Cleared	NCSM Decision	Activation Indicator	Activation Status
CBU-001	VJDH	84%	Yes	Pending	☐	Not Activated
After NCSM approval:
CBU	Invoicing Depot	MSTN %	MSTN Cleared	NCSM Decision	Activation Indicator	Activation Status
CBU-001	VJDH	84%	Yes	Approved	✓	Active
Result:
•	VJDH becomes available in CCR.
•	Basepack transitions from Partially Active to Active if all remaining depots are activated.
•	Activation of VJDH does not require reactivation of already approved depots.
This flow will be incorporated into service request approval flow documented in Section 10.5
